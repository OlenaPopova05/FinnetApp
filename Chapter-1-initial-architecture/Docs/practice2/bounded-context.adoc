= Contracts Module - Bounded Context Description

The Contracts module represents a bounded context responsible for managing
the lifecycle of gym service agreements between customers and the gym.
It encapsulates all business rules related to contract preparation,
validation and signing.

== Responsibilities

* Preparing contracts for customers before they can use gym services
* Validating customer eligibility based on business rules (age, height, previous contract status)
* Managing the contract signing process
* Tracking contract lifecycle 
* Enforcing contract business rules such as:
** Previous contracts must be signed before preparing new ones
** Contracts can only be signed within 30 days of preparation

== Not Responsible For

* Managing customer personal information (this belongs to Customers module)
* Processing payments or billing (this belongs to Payments module)
* Managing gym passes or access control (this belongs to Passes module)
* Generating offers or promotions (this belongs to Offers module)
* Creating reports or analytics (this belongs to Reports module)

== Data Ownership

The Contracts module has Contract entity with the following attributes:

** `Id` - unique contract identifier
** `CustomerId` - reference to customer
** `PreparedAt` - when the contract was prepared
** `Duration` - contract validity period
** `SignedAt` - when the contract was signed
** `ExpiringAt` - when the contract expires
** `Signed` - contract status
